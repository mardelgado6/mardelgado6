## 🙋‍♀️ About me

I'm an AI solutions leader with seven years of experience in retail and financial services (El Puerto de Liverpool and Claro Pay). I combine a solid technical foundation — M.Sc. in Data Science, B.Sc. in Actuarial Science, Machine Learning and Google Cloud — with a business-first mindset.

I believe technology should open doors for people and their businesses: helping teams get unstuck from day-to-day operations, free up time and grow.

## 🚪 Efatá AI

In April 2026 I founded **[Efatá AI](https://efataai.com/)**, where we design and implement solutions in four areas:

- 🤖 **Applied AI** — generative AI, LLMs, agents and agentic AI
- ⚙️ **Automation** — workflows that remove operational bottlenecks
- 📊 **Data Analytics** — turning business data into clear, actionable decisions
- 📣 **Digital Marketing** — strategy and content that connect brands with their customers

We've delivered solutions for clients in the restaurant, agro-industrial, healthcare and retail sectors, working hand in hand with each business owner to define scope, priorities and results. I lead a team of three specialists.
